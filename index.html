Inside the frame

What goes into a VTech window.

  1. 01

    Multi-chambered uPVC profile

    Hollow, multi-chambered profiles with segregated drainage. Frame wall approx. 2.0 mm.

  2. 02

    Galvanised steel reinforcement

    Steel shaped for structural strength sits inside the profile chamber, 1.2 to 3.0 mm on outward-opening windows.

  3. 03

    EPDM weather and glazing seals

    Seals around the sash and glass, with pile seals that help keep out dust.

  4. 04

    Mitred beads, single or double glazing

    Mitred glazing beads give a cleaner finish; single or double glazing is available.

  5. 05

    Corrosion-resistant hardware

    Fittings that resist atmospheric corrosion, with single- or multi-point locks.

    Why uPVC

    Built to be lived with, not looked after.

    • Thermal insulation

      uPVC profiles offer good thermal insulation thanks to low thermal conductivity.

    • Noise insulation

      VTech lists strong insulation against ambient noise as a core benefit of uPVC.

    • Weather and light resistance

      Unaffected by climate changes and resistant to fading from light; will not rot or warp.

    • Low maintenance

      No sanding, painting or varnishing. Smooth frames clean with dishwashing liquid.

    • Galvanised steel reinforcement

      Steel inside the hollow chambers adds strength and durability to uPVC frames.

    • Recyclable

      VTech states uPVC is 100% recyclable, with lower production energy use than wood.
